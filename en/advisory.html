ICC Learning Expeditions™ · Executive programmes

Learning by doing, breaking with the familiar

Tailor-made immersive experiences for leaders, leadership teams, institutions, companies and regions engaged in deep transformation.

Far more than study trips, they are an experiential module of the ICC Transformation Architecture™. Each expedition is built around the organisation’s strategic challenges and designed as a living lab that fosters encounters, learning and new forms of cooperation.

  1. High-level meetings
  2. Immersive visits
  3. Collaborative workshops
  4. Strategic reflection time

An international network mobilised for each journey:

  • Universities
  • Institutions
  • Pioneering companies
  • Living Labs
  • Research centres
  • International organisations
  • Innovation ecosystems
